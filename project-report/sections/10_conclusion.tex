\subsection*{10.1 Introduction}
The CypherRoll project was conceptualized and developed to address the acute transparency, fairness, and custodial challenges present in conventional online casinos by introducing a decentralized, secure, and provably fair hybrid Web3 gaming platform. This chapter summarizes the overall outcomes, technical achievements, broader socio-economic impact, and future enhancement roadmap of the system.

\subsection*{10.2 Summary of the Project}
CypherRoll provides an enterprise-grade blockchain platform that transforms digital gambling from an opaque black-box into a verifiable, non-custodial gaming ecosystem. By synthesizing off-chain real-time 3D WebGL game execution with on-chain Ethereum smart contract settlement, the system overcomes the latency and fee limitations of traditional decentralized applications while preserving 100\% mathematical auditability.

The platform includes features such as gas-free Web3 authentication through MetaMask (SIWE - EIP-4361), cryptographic commit-reveal provable fairness via HMAC-SHA256, non-custodial escrow vaults (\texttt{CypherRollVault.sol}), and instant EIP-712 operator-authorized payouts. These components work together to deliver an authentic, entertaining, and transparent gaming experience.

\subsection*{10.3 Achievements of the System}
The CypherRoll system successfully achieved all of its primary and specific engineering objectives:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item \textbf{Mathematical Provable Fairness:} Designed and verified an open commit-reveal protocol with zero deviations across extensive Monte Carlo testing ($N = 10,000$ rounds).
    \item \textbf{Non-Custodial Smart Contract Escrow:} Deployed and validated the \texttt{CypherRollVault.sol} contract across Base Sepolia and Ethereum Sepolia, ensuring that players retain self-custody over their funds.
    \item \textbf{High-Speed 3D WebGL Graphics:} Implemented custom Three.js environments delivering fluid 60 FPS physics-driven dice rolling and rocket crash flights.
    \item \textbf{Instant Cryptographic Payouts:} Successfully integrated EIP-712 typed structured data signing, allowing players to autonomously claim balances on-chain without human intermediary delay.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{10.4 Impact of the System}
The CypherRoll platform has a significant potential impact on both digital entertainment and decentralized finance:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item \textbf{Restoring User Trust:} By replacing blind faith with open mathematical proofs, the platform establishes a new benchmark for integrity in online wagering.
    \item \textbf{Empowering Player Custody:} By replacing centralized corporate bank accounts with transparent smart contracts, the system eliminates counterparty risk and arbitrary asset lockups.
    \item \textbf{Demonstrating Layer-2 Viability:} The project proves that modern Layer-2 rollups like Base make micro-wagers and frequent gaming transactions economically feasible with sub-cent gas fees.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{10.5 Limitations}
Despite its significant advantages, the system has certain real-world limitations:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item \textbf{Public RPC Throttling:} High-frequency deposit verification on public testnet RPCs can experience rate limiting without dedicated RPC infrastructure.
    \item \textbf{Gas Volatility on L1:} While Layer-2 deployments are extremely cheap, settlements directly on Ethereum Layer-1 remain subject to network congestion and variable gas prices.
\end{itemize}
